SECTION G: INSTRUCTIONS FOR COMPLETION
▪ One form per shift change. The form travels with the Permit to Work.
▪ Complete physically on site, at the work face, with lighting on for a night handover — never from the site office.
▪ The incoming supervisor may refuse to accept the shift and escalate to the Site Manager if any Section B line is unsafe or incomplete.
▪ Retain the signed form with the PTW and the Daily EHS Report for the project retention period.
▪ A copy of every completed handover is filed against KAZ-EHS-MS-2026-004 Annex A.
