### SLIDE 2: Observation #01 – Unauthorized Work – Executing Site Activities Without an Approved Permit to Work (PTW)

**Slide Title:** Observation #01: Unauthorized Work – Execution Without Approved Permit to Work (PTW)

**Non-Compliance Details:**
- Subcontractor workforce observed executing high-risk activities (mechanical installation and excavation) without a valid, approved Permit to Work.
- Direct violation of Siemens Energy Instruction: EHS General Requirements for Contractors (Section 5.3 – PTW System) and KAZ Project EHS Plan Section 7.2.
- No evidence of Task Risk Assessment (TRA) review, energy isolation verification, or Simultaneous Operations (SIMOPS) coordination.
- PTW logbook not maintained at site office; permits not displayed at work front as per procedure. Workers unaware of permit conditions.

**Associated Risk Level:** **CRITICAL**
- Potential for fatality, uncontrolled release of hazardous energy, fire/explosion, and SIMOPS conflict.
- Represents total loss of primary control barrier – a leading indicator of major accident potential (MAH).
- Breach of ISO 45001:2018 Clause 8.1.2 – Eliminating hazards and reducing OH&S risks.
- Legal exposure: Regulatory authority may issue prohibition notice.

**Required Corrective Action:**
1. **IMMEDIATE STOP WORK** order for all activities without PTW – enforce until full compliance verified by SE EHS.
2. Mandatory re-training of all subcontractor supervision (foremen, engineers) on Siemens Energy PTW System within 24 hours – attendance sheet + competency test.
3. Initiate formal Non-Conformance Report (NCR) and contractual warning letter – Level 1.
4. EHS to conduct full PTW system audit and daily spot-checks for next 14 days – findings to be included in daily report.
5. Supervisor accountability: Removal of non-compliant supervisors if recurrence observed – implement 3-strikes policy.

### SLIDE 5: Observation #04 – Excavation Safety – Inadequate Hard Barricades and Physical Protection

**Slide Title:** Observation #04: Excavation Safety – Inadequate Hard Barricades & Physical Protection

**Non-Compliance Details:**
- Excavation zones >1.2m depth (up to 2.5m observed) without hard barricades; only loose warning tape used, which is prohibited for excavations >1.2m.

- Non-compliance with OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P, ISO 45001 Working at Height/Excavation, and Siemens Energy Excavation Safety Standard SE-GEN-EHS-007.
- Night-time visibility absent – no reflective markers, delineators, or adequate lighting.

**Associated Risk Level:** **CRITICAL**

3. Excavation permit re-validation with geotechnical check and competent person inspection.

5. Competent person inspection checklist to be submitted daily before start of work – signed by subcontractor engineer and verified by SE EHS.

### SLIDE 10: Observation #09 – Hazardous Material Storage – Improper Isolation, Lack of Secondary Containment in Diesel Storage Area

**Slide Title:** Observation #09: Hazardous Material Storage – Diesel Storage Without Secondary Containment

**Non-Compliance Details:**
- Diesel storage area (approx. 2000L in 2x IBC tanks) observed without secondary containment/bunding – 110% capacity required per standard.
- No isolation distance from ignition sources (welding 10m away); no fire extinguisher, no HAZCHEM signage, no spill kit.
- Storage on bare ground – no impervious base, no earthing/bonding for fuel transfer – static electricity risk.
- Direct violation of HSG 176 (Storage of Flammable Liquids), EPA Spill Prevention, Control and Countermeasure (SPCC), and SE Hazardous Materials Standard SE-ENV-003.

**Associated Risk Level:** **CRITICAL**
- Major fire/explosion risk, catastrophic environmental release, soil/groundwater contamination leading to regulatory prosecution and potential imprisonment.
- Potential for Major Accident Hazard (MAH) – pool fire affecting adjacent works.
- Immediate danger to life and environment – highest priority.

**Required Corrective Action:**
1. STOP all fueling operations – secure area with fire watch and 15m exclusion zone until compliant.
2. Provide compliant bunded storage – 110% secondary containment, impervious concrete floor, roofed, ventilated, 15m from drains/buildings.
3. Install fire extinguishers (2x 9kg DCP + 1x foam), HAZCHEM signage, spill kit (150L capacity), and earthing/bonding system – tested.
4. Develop and communicate Fuel Storage & Handling SOP and conduct emergency response drill (fuel spill + fire).
5. Location to be approved by SE EHS – minimum 15m from excavations, buildings, drains, and ignition sources – risk assessment required.
